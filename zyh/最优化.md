### ZYH

Implementation of several optimization methods covered in the Lecture "Fundamentals of Optimization"

The corresponding theoretical foundations (in german) are detailed at <https://scoop.iwr.uni-heidelberg.de/teaching/2026ws/lecture-grundlagen-der-optimierung/>

uses xxds for matrices, vectors, their operations

##### unrestrained:

- gradient descent
- preconditioned steepest descent
- quasi-nweton method
- newton algorithm

##### linear

- simplex algorithm
- interior point methods

##### convex

- bundle methods
